Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

1 Introduction

TraceCam ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information and camera feeds when you use our app.

2 Local Camera & Photo Processing

Your privacy is our primary priority. All camera feeds and photo selections are processed locally on your device:

  • Camera Feed: Camera access is used exclusively to display a real-time overlay view over physical paper. The live feed is never recorded, captured, or transmitted off your device.
  • No Photo Uploads: Photos selected from your gallery to convert into line sketches are processed entirely on your phone. We do not operate external image processing servers.
  • No Storage: Converted sketches exist only within the temporary memory of the application while you draw and are never stored or uploaded in the cloud.

3 Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data. The only information we may temporarily receive is:

  • Support Contact: If you choose to contact us directly via email, we receive your email address and any details you provide. This information is only used to assist you and is deleted once your request is resolved.

4 Third-Party Services (AdMob)

This app displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob. To serve relevant ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and use certain non-personal data. This can include your device advertising ID, IP address, and basic device metrics.

Please note: AdMob is an independent third-party provider. Their data collection and usage are governed directly by their own privacy policies. We highly recommend reviewing how they handle user data here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

5 Your Control

Because all image conversion and camera operations remain strictly local to your device, you can revoke camera or gallery permissions at any time through your Android system settings, or clear all app data by uninstalling the app.

6 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any modifications will be updated on this page with a revised publication date.
